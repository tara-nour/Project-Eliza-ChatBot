# ROI Analysis: MédiRDV
> **Formula:** `ROI = (Profit / Capital Invested) × 100%`

---

| Cost Item | Amount |
|---|---|

## Gains Generated — Backed by Real Data

### 1. Receptionist Time Saved

> *Source: ERI Salary Survey — France*

The average medical receptionist in France earns **€14/hour**. A receptionist typically spends ~2.5 hours/day on scheduling calls. MédiRDV automates ~**70%** of that workload:

```
2.5h × 70% × 220 working days × €14 = €5,390 saved/year
```

---

### 2. Reduction in Missed Appointments

> *Source: Conferbot / Accenture 2025 — Connexion France (Dec 2024)*

AI scheduling chatbots have been shown to reduce no-shows by **15–25%** through automated reminders. Applying a conservative **25% reduction**, with a GP consultation now at **€30** (since December 2024), and an estimated 12 no-shows/month:

```
12 × 25% × 12 months × €30 = €1,080 recovered/year
```

---

### 3. Extended Booking Hours = More Appointments

> *Source: MGMA — Weill Cornell Medicine case study*

Weill Cornell Medicine reported a **47% increase** in appointments booked digitally after deploying an AI chatbot for 24/7 scheduling. Applying a conservative **20% uplift** on bookings:

```
2 extra bookings/day × 5 days × €30 × 48 weeks = €14,400 additional revenue/year
```

---

### 4. Growing Market Tailwind

> *Source: Grand View Research — Conferbot 2025*

The global healthcare chatbot market was valued at **$787 million in 2024** and is projected to reach **$2.4 billion by 2027** — validating the long-term commercial viability and scalability of MédiRDV.
